Tax preparation should feel clear, predictable, and calm. Here is how TAXEM approaches your return.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about our local-first architecture and civic principles.

Is TAXEM online or offline?

It is designed to run entirely locally. The website you are viewing now is a promo showcase, but the actual TAXEM engine compiles and runs on your machine. Your financial information never uploads to our servers.

Why do you emphasize Quebec?

Quebec has a separate, highly distinct provincial tax filing system. Most software treats it as an afterthought. We built our architecture to natively support dual-jurisdiction coupling from the ground up.

Is TAXEM certified by the CRA and Revenu Québec?

No, TAXEM is currently a development-stage prototype and is not yet certified for direct electronic transmission (NETFILE) by the CRA or Revenu Québec. We are actively organizing our calculations and structuring layouts to prepare for formal certification cycles through official channels in a future release.

What tax years and provinces are supported?

TAXEM currently supports calculations for the 2024 and 2025 tax years. Our active certification direction is Ontario-first, which supports Ontario residents filing individual returns (T1 with Form ON428 and ON479). The Quebec TP-1 calculation engine is present in our code for dual-jurisdiction future-proofing, but provincial Quebec filing is currently unsupported. For other provinces, calculations are present in the core but not yet fully wired to the user interface. Read our detailed Scope & Limitations page for more details.

How is TAXEM structured?

The platform is grounded in civic technology principles. All supported calculation features, schedules, and credits are fully accessible from day one, without paywalls for deductions or unexpected upgrade prompts.
